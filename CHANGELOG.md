# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-07-21

First public release.

- Toggle Like with a configurable key on videos and Shorts.
- Seek backward and forward in Shorts.
- Open, close, and scroll the Shorts comments panel from the keyboard.
- Customize all shortcuts, seek duration, scroll amount, smooth scrolling, and feature toggles.
- Sync preferences through Chromium extension storage.
- Ignore shortcuts while typing.
- Show no injected popups or notifications.
